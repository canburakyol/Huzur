# Huzur App Localization & UX Copy Audit Report

As a senior localization expert and UX writer, I have audited all language files to ensure the application maintains a calming, empathetic, and professional tone consistent with the "Huzur" philosophy.

## Summary of Interventions
- **Removed Aggressive Punctuation**: Replaced exclamation marks (`!`) with soft periods (`.`) or removed them entirely to avoid a "yelling" or overly urgent tone.
- **Softened Terminology**: Replaced harsh words like "Error", "Failed", "Delete", "Hata", "Başarısız", "Sil" with gentler alternatives like "Issue", "Could not complete", "Remove", "Sorun", "Tamamlanamadı", "Kaldır".
- **UI Consistency**: Ensured action buttons use softer, consistent verbs.
