## Positioning

Primary promise:

> Build a calm daily Islamic worship routine with prayer times, Quran, dua, and dhikr in one place.

Do not lead with:

- "Muslim super app"
- "AI Islamic assistant"
- "Everything for Muslims"
- "Full Quran app"

## Recommended Main Store Listing

### App Title

Huzur: Daily Islamic Routine

### Short Description

Track prayer times and build a calm daily routine with Quran, dua, and dhikr.

### Full Description

Huzur helps you build a simple daily Islamic worship routine without turning faith into a complicated checklist.

Follow accurate prayer times, keep calm reminders, and choose one short step for today: Quran, dua, dhikr, or prayer tracking.

The goal is not to do everything at once. The goal is to not let today pass empty.

With Huzur, you can:

- Follow daily prayer times based on your location.
- Keep gentle reminders for salah and daily worship.
- Start a short Quran, dua, or dhikr step each day.
- Use essential Islamic tools such as qibla, tasbih, duas, and zakat.
- Track your daily rhythm and return when you miss a day.
- Upgrade to Pro for deeper routine support, weekly insight, and an ad-free focus experience.

Huzur is designed for Muslims who want a calmer, more consistent connection with worship.

Start with one clear step today.

- Language: English

Do not create separate country copy yet unless Play Console data shows meaningful visitor volume in a specific country.

## Measurement Rule

Judge the English listing with the following order:

1. Store listing visitors by country and language
2. Store listing conversion rate
3. Acquisition source
4. First open and onboarding completion
5. D1 and D7 retention
6. Review language and complaint themes

Do not judge the launch by install count alone. If visitors are low, the issue is distribution. If visitors are healthy but conversion is low, the issue is listing copy, screenshots, trust, or localization mismatch.
